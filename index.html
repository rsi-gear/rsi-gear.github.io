01 / The frameworkWithin tasks · Across tasks

Learn from experience.
Improve across tasks.

GEAR organizes recursive self-improvement as a two-level meta-learning process. In the inner loop, a rollout agent uses task feedback to refine its reasoning, planning, and actions within a task.

In the outer loop, a Meta Agent identifies common patterns across task experiences, including recurring failures and effective strategies, and proposes reusable improvements to enhance cross-task performance. GEAR evaluates and promotes these changes for subsequent tasks. The two loops can be optimized independently and are connected by experience.
